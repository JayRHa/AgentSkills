# Lawful Basis Guide (GDPR Art. 6)

Every processing purpose needs **exactly one** of these six bases. Pick the most honest fit.

| Basis | Use when | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Consent** (6(1)(a)) | The person has a genuine free choice and you can prove opt-in | Must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous, and **withdrawable** as easily as given. Not valid where there's a power imbalance (employer/employee). |
| **Contract** (6(1)(b)) | Processing is necessary to deliver a contract the person is party to (or pre-contract steps) | Only covers what's genuinely necessary to perform the contract, not "nice to have". |
| **Legal obligation** (6(1)(c)) | A law requires the processing (tax, AML, employment records) | Cite the specific obligation. |
| **Vital interests** (6(1)(d)) | To protect someone's life | Rare; emergencies only. |
| **Public task** (6(1)(e)) | Exercise of official authority / task in the public interest | Mainly public bodies. |
| **Legitimate interests** (6(1)(f)) | Your (or a third party's) interest, not overridden by the person's rights | Requires a **Legitimate Interest Assessment** (purpose → necessity → balancing). Not available to public authorities for their tasks. |

## Decision flow

1. Is processing legally required? → **Legal obligation**.
2. Needed to perform a contract with this person? → **Contract**.
3. Life at risk? → **Vital interests**.
4. Public-authority task? → **Public task**.
5. Otherwise: can you justify it against the person's rights with an LIA? → **Legitimate interests**.
6. None of the above, and you can offer real choice? → **Consent**.

## Special category data (Art. 9)

For health, biometric, genetic, racial/ethnic, political, religious, trade-union, sex-life data you need an Art. 6 basis **plus** an Art. 9 condition (e.g. explicit consent, employment law, substantial public interest). Flag these activities for extra scrutiny.

## Document for each activity
- Chosen basis + one-line justification
- For consent: where/when captured, and how withdrawal works
- For legitimate interest: link to the LIA
- For special category: the Art. 9 condition relied on
