# Worked Example: 4-Day Hypertrophy Program

## Profile
Intermediate lifter, **4 days/week**, ~60 min/session, **barbell + dumbbells + bench/rack**, goal **muscle growth**, no injuries. Cleared for training.

## Split: Upper / Lower ×2 (each muscle 2×/week)
Mon Upper A · Tue Lower A · Thu Upper B · Fri Lower B · Wed/Sat/Sun rest or light cardio.

### Upper A
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Progression |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell bench press | 4 × 6–8 | 2 min | Double progression |
| Bent-over row | 4 × 8–10 | 2 min | Double progression |
| Seated DB shoulder press | 3 × 8–12 | 90 s | Add reps then load |
| Lat pulldown / pull-up | 3 × 8–12 | 90 s | — |
| DB lateral raise | 3 × 12–15 | 60 s | — |
| Triceps pushdown + DB curl (superset) | 3 × 10–15 | 60 s | — |

### Lower A
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Back squat | 4 × 6–8 | 3 min |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8–10 | 2 min |
| Walking lunge | 3 × 10/leg | 90 s |
| Leg curl | 3 × 12–15 | 60 s |
| Standing calf raise | 4 × 12–15 | 60 s |
| Hanging leg raise | 3 × 10–15 | 60 s |

### Upper B / Lower B
Mirror A with variation: incline DB press, weighted pull-up, overhead press, cable row, hammer curls (Upper B); front squat or leg press, hip thrust, Bulgarian split squat, seated calf, plank (Lower B).

## Progression rule
Double progression on all compounds: when you hit the **top** of the rep range for **all** sets, add ~2.5 kg (upper) / 5 kg (lower) next session and reset to the bottom of the range.

## Deload
Week 6: drop to ~2 sets per exercise at the same load, then resume.

## Tracking
Log each session: `exercise — load × sets × reps @ RPE`. Aim to beat last week's reps or load on at least the first compound each day.

> **Disclaimer:** General fitness guidance, not medical advice. Clear new training with a physician if you have any health condition or injury; stop on sharp pain.
