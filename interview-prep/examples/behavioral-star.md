# Worked Example: Raw Experience → STAR Answer

## Question
"Tell me about a time you had to deliver under a tight deadline with limited resources."

## Candidate's raw notes
"We had a big client demo and our main dev quit. I had to get it done with just two people and not much time. We made it and the client signed."

## Coached STAR answer
> **(Situation)** Two weeks before a make-or-break demo for our largest prospective client — a deal worth about $400k ARR — our lead engineer resigned, leaving just me and one junior developer.
>
> **(Task)** I owned delivery of a working integration demo on the original date; pushing it risked losing the client to a competitor already in talks.
>
> **(Action)** I cut scope to the three workflows the client actually cared about and shelved the rest. I paired with the junior dev each morning to unblock them fast, and I personally took the riskiest integration. I set a daily 15-minute checkpoint with our sales lead so expectations stayed aligned, and I built a manual fallback for the one piece I wasn't sure we'd finish.
>
> **(Result)** We delivered all three core workflows on time. The demo ran clean, the client signed a $400k contract two weeks later, and the scoped-out features became our next sprint. I learned that aggressive scope-cutting plus daily unblocking beats heroics — and I now default to that under time pressure.

## Interviewer-style feedback delivered to the candidate
- ✅ Strong: clear stakes ($400k), "I" not "we" in the Actions, quantified result, a reflection at the end.
- 🔧 Tighten: the Situation is slightly long — you can drop the competitor detail if time-pressed.
- 🔧 Add: name one concrete trade-off you said *no* to — it shows judgment.
- ⏱️ Length: ~75 seconds spoken. Good.
