# Question Bank

## Universals (prepare all of these)
- Tell me about yourself.
- Why do you want this role / this company?
- Why are you leaving your current role?
- What's your greatest strength? Greatest weakness?
- Where do you see yourself in 3–5 years?
- What are your salary expectations?
- Do you have any questions for us?

## Behavioral (pick stories from your bank)
- Tell me about a time you led a project / a team.
- Describe a conflict with a coworker and how you resolved it.
- Tell me about a failure and what you learned.
- A time you handled ambiguity or shifting priorities.
- A time you influenced a decision without formal authority.
- A time you went above and beyond / handled an unhappy stakeholder.
- A time you received tough feedback.

## Role-specific (derive from the JD)
For each top requirement in the job description, expect one question. Examples:
- *PM:* "How do you prioritize a roadmap with conflicting stakeholders?"
- *Engineer:* "Walk me through a hard bug you debugged."
- *Sales:* "Tell me about your largest deal and how you closed it."
- *Designer:* "Walk me through a design decision you'd defend."

## Strong questions to ask back
- "What does success look like in this role at 30/60/90 days?"
- "What's the biggest challenge the team is facing right now?"
- "How is performance measured, and how often?"
- "What's the team's working style and decision-making process?"
- "Why is this role open?"
- "What do you enjoy most about working here?"

## Avoid asking (too early)
Salary/benefits/vacation in a first technical round, anything answerable from the careers page, "what does the company do?"
