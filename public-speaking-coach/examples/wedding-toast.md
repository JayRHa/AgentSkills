# Worked Example: Best-Man Toast

## Brief
Best man, ~3 minutes, 80 guests, warm and a little funny. Worried about rambling and nerves. Rough notes: "Known Tom since college. He's loyal, a terrible cook, met Sara and changed. Want to wish them well."

## Core message
*Tom found in Sara the one person who makes him better — and that's worth celebrating.*

## Structure (relationship → anecdote → turn → wish)
1. **Open (hook):** a specific, funny image — not "For those who don't know me…"
2. **Who Tom is:** loyalty, with one quick concrete proof.
3. **The turn:** how Sara changed him (the heart of it).
4. **Wish + toast:** raise the glass on the core message.

## The toast (~2.5 min spoken)

> Good evening. For those who don't know me — I'm Dan, and I'm the friend Tom calls at 2 a.m. when his "experimental" cooking sets off the smoke alarm. Which is how I know exactly how brave Sara is.
>
> I met Tom in our first week of college, both of us pretending we knew how laundry worked. Twelve years later, here's what I can tell you: Tom is the most loyal person I know. When my dad was in the hospital, Tom drove four hours, sat with me all night, and never once made it about him. That's who he is.
>
> But I'll be honest — the Tom from college was a bit of a mess. *(pause)* And then he met Sara. I noticed it slowly: he started showing up on time. He learned to listen. He got *better* — not because she asked him to, but because he wanted to be the kind of man who deserved her. Sara, you did what four roommates and one very concerned mother could not. *(let the laugh land)*
>
> Tom, you found the one person who makes you the best version of yourself. That's the whole thing. That's everything.
>
> So please, raise your glasses. To Tom and Sara — to a life of getting better, together. *(raise)* Cheers.

## Why it works
- One **core message**, set up early and paid off at the toast.
- **One specific story** (the hospital) earns the emotion; one running joke (the cooking) earns the laughs — not ten inside jokes.
- A clear **turn** (Sara's effect) that makes it about the couple, not the best man.
- **Bookended** open/close, under time, ends on a clean raise-the-glass line.

## Delivery notes given to the speaker
- Mark the three `(pause)` spots — let the laughs and the emotional line breathe.
- Rehearse the open and the final toast line until automatic; that's where nerves hit.
- Hold the glass *before* the last line so your hands have a job.
