# Travel Budgeting Reference

Use this to convert a total budget into category allocations and to sanity-check whether a budget is tight, comfortable, or generous for a destination.

## Step 1 — Decide what the budget includes
Always confirm scope. Common scopes:
- **All-in**: flights + lodging + on-the-ground.
- **On-the-ground only**: lodging + food + activities + local transit (flights bought separately).

Plan against the scope the user states. If unstated, assume **all-in** and say so.

## Step 2 — Category split heuristics (all-in trip)
These are starting splits, not rules. Adjust for trip type.

| Category | Budget trip | Mid-range | Comfort/Luxury |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transport (flights + intercity) | 35% | 30% | 25% |
| Lodging | 25% | 30% | 35% |
| Food | 18% | 18% | 18% |
| Activities & entry | 10% | 10% | 12% |
| Local transit | 4% | 4% | 3% |
| Buffer / contingency | 8% | 8% | 7% |

Notes:
- **Road trips**: move money from flights to fuel + car rental + tolls (still under Transport).
- **Beach/resort**: lodging often swallows 40%+ (all-inclusive folds food/activities in).
- **Backpacking**: lodging drops, food/local-transit rise; buffer stays.

## Step 3 — Daily on-the-ground spend ranges
Rough per-person, per-day, excluding flights and lodging. Wide ranges because cities vary; use the midpoint as a default and adjust.

| Region | Budget/day | Mid/day | Comfort/day |
|---|---|---|---|
| SE Asia (Thailand, Vietnam) | $25–40 | $50–90 | $150+ |
| South Asia (India, Nepal) | $20–35 | $40–80 | $130+ |
| Eastern Europe | $40–60 | $70–120 | $200+ |
| Western Europe | $70–110 | $130–220 | $350+ |
| Japan / South Korea | $60–95 | $110–190 | $320+ |
| USA (major cities) | $80–130 | $150–260 | $400+ |
| Latin America | $35–60 | $70–130 | $250+ |
| Australia / NZ | $75–120 | $140–240 | $380+ |

Lodging per night (double room, mid-range) typically runs 1–2x the mid daily on-the-ground figure in the same region.

## Step 4 — Verdict logic
Compute available on-the-ground per day = (budget − flights − lodging) / days / travelers. Compare to the region table:
- Below budget column → **tight**: recommend hostels/guesthouses, street food, free attractions, fewer paid tours.
- Within mid column → **comfortable**.
- Above comfort column → **generous**: room for splurge meals, private tours, better lodging.

## Step 5 — Trade-down ladder (when over budget)
Apply in this order; stop when reconciled:
1. **Lodging tier** — biggest lever; one tier down often saves 20–40% of lodging.
2. **Paid attractions** — swap to free/cheaper equivalents (parks, viewpoints, free museum days).
3. **Dining tier** — shift some sit-down meals to markets/casual; keep 1–2 signature meals.
4. **Local transport** — transit passes vs. taxis/rideshare.
5. **Trip length** — last resort; cut a day rather than ruin the pace.

## Hidden costs to always include
- Airport/station transfers (both ends).
- Tourist taxes / city taxes (per night in many EU cities).
- Tips (region-dependent — research norms).
- SIM/eSIM or roaming.
- Travel insurance.
- Reservation/booking fees and timed-entry premiums.
- ATM/foreign-transaction fees.
- Luggage fees on budget airlines.

## Quick rule of thumb
If you can't itemize, estimate on-the-ground cost as: `days × travelers × regional mid-rate`, add lodging, add flights, then add a 12% buffer. Refine from there.
