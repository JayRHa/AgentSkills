# Worked Example: Gift for Dad

## Input
"Birthday gift for my dad, turning 60. Budget ~$150. He loves cooking (grilling especially), gardening, and dad-rock vinyl. Recently mentioned his knives are dull. Hates clutter and gadgets he won't use."

## Profile signals
- **Hobbies:** grilling, gardening, vinyl.
- **Daily annoyance (gold):** dull knives — he said it himself.
- **Constraint:** anti-clutter, anti-gadget → avoid single-use gizmos.
- **Milestone:** 60th → can lean slightly more meaningful/premium.

## Ranked recommendations

**1. Professional knife sharpening + a quality sharpening stone — ~$60 (safe·high-fit)**
Solves the exact annoyance he named. Pair the stone with a one-time pro sharpening of his existing knives. Useful, no clutter, shows you listened. *Top pick.*

**2. A premium grilling experience or class — ~$120 (bold·memorable)**
A local BBQ/smoking masterclass or a steak-tasting. Experience over object, matches his passion, nothing to store. Bonus if you go together.

**3. Reissue vinyl of a favorite album + a record-cleaning kit — ~$50 (safe·sentimental)**
Pick a specific album tied to a memory; add a note about why. Feeds an existing hobby, low clutter.

**4. High-quality heirloom garden tool — ~$90 (safe·practical-delightful)**
A forged hand trowel/secateurs that will outlast cheap ones — an upgrade of something he uses constantly.

## Combo suggestion (≈$150)
**Knife sharpening + stone (#1, ~$60)** as the practical "I listened" gift **+ the reissue vinyl (#3, ~$50)** as the sentimental piece, with a handwritten note tied to the album — leaving ~$40 buffer. Two angles (useful + meaningful) within budget.

## Fallback
If timing is tight: a gift card to his favorite butcher/grill shop + the vinyl, with a note. Low risk, on-theme.

## Presentation
Wrap the stone with the note tucked inside; "voucher" the pro sharpening on a nice card so there's something to open.

> Confirm current prices/availability and album pressing before buying.
