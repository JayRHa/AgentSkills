# Negotiation Scripts

Stay warm, specific, and collaborative. Use "we", tie asks to reasons, then go quiet.

## When asked for expectations early (deflect)
> "I'd love to learn more about the role's scope first. Do you have a budgeted range you're working with? I'm confident we can find a number that works."

## If forced to give a number
> "Based on my research for this role and level in [location], I'm targeting the [X–Y] range, and I'm flexible depending on the full package."

## Countering a written offer (the core move)
> "Thank you — I'm genuinely excited about this role and the team. Based on my experience with [specific scope] and market data for the level, I was hoping we could bring the base to **$X**. Is there flexibility there?"

Then **stop talking.**

## Bundling multiple asks
> "I'm on board with the direction. Three things would make this an easy yes: base at $X, the signing bonus at $Y to offset the bonus I'm forfeiting, and a start date of [date]. Which of these can we work with?"

## Using a competing offer (honestly)
> "I have another offer at $X total comp, but [Company] is my first choice. If we can get closer to that number, I'm ready to sign."

## Asking for a raise (internal)
> "Over the past year I [impact: shipped X, owned Y, saved Z]. Market data for this scope is around $X, which is above my current band. I'd like to discuss bringing my comp in line with that. What would it take to get there?"

## If they say it's final
> "I understand there are constraints. If base is fixed, could we look at a signing bonus or a comp review at six months tied to clear goals?"

## Closing
> "That works for me — thank you for working through it. Could you send the updated offer in writing so I can review and sign?"

## Tone rules
- Never apologize for negotiating.
- Never make it personal or adversarial.
- One clear ask at a time (or one bundle), then silence.
- Confirm every agreed change **in writing** before resigning anything.
