# Worked Example: Before → After

## Context
Candidate: support engineer applying for a **Customer Success Manager** role. Job description emphasizes *retention, onboarding, cross-functional work, and data*.

## Bullet rewrites

**Before:** Responsible for helping customers with their problems and answering tickets.
**After:** Resolved ~45 support tickets/week with a 96% CSAT, and built a self-serve FAQ that **cut repeat tickets 30%**.

**Before:** Worked on onboarding new clients.
**After:** Onboarded **60+ enterprise clients**, designing a 30-day onboarding playbook that **lifted 90-day activation from 55% → 78%**.

**Before:** Helped reduce churn.
**After:** Partnered with Product and Sales to launch a churn-risk alert; **reduced quarterly churn from 7% → 4.5%** across a $2M book of business.

## Summary rewrite

**Before:**
> Results-oriented professional and team player seeking a challenging new role to leverage my skills.

**After:**
> Customer-facing support engineer moving into Customer Success. 3 years turning support insights into retention: onboarded 60+ enterprise accounts and cut churn from 7% to 4.5%. Strong with Zendesk, SQL, and cross-functional rollout.

## What changed
- Every line now has a **number and an outcome**.
- Language mirrors the JD: *onboarding, retention, churn, cross-functional, data*.
- The summary states **direction** (the pivot) and **proof**, not adjectives.

## Gap analysis delivered to the user
> The JD also wants "experience with Gainsight." You haven't used it. Either add it if you've touched it, or note in your cover letter that you've used Zendesk + SQL for the same workflows and can ramp quickly.
