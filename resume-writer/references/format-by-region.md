# Resume Format by Region

| Region | Name | Length | Photo? | Personal data | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US / Canada | Resume | 1 page (<10 yrs), 2 max | **No** | No age/marital/photo | Anti-discrimination norms; omit personal data |
| UK / Ireland | CV | 2 pages | No | No | "CV" but means resume; references "on request" |
| Germany / Austria / Switzerland (DACH) | Lebenslauf | 1–2 pages | Often yes | DOB common, signature sometimes | Tabular, very structured; often expects a cover letter (Anschreiben) |
| France | CV | 1 page | Optional | Limited | Concise, formal |
| Academic (any) | Academic CV | No limit | No | No | Publications, grants, teaching, talks; reverse-chron within sections |
| Australia / NZ | Resume/CV | 2–3 pages | No | No | Slightly longer than US norm |

## Section order (reverse-chronological, default)
1. Name + contact (+ LinkedIn/portfolio)
2. Professional summary
3. Skills (for tech/ATS-heavy roles, can sit high)
4. Experience (most recent first)
5. Education
6. Certifications / Projects / Publications (as relevant)

## When to deviate
- **New grads:** Education and Projects above Experience.
- **Career changers:** lead with a summary + transferable-skills block.
- **DACH applications:** follow the tabular Lebenslauf convention and expect to include a photo and DOB unless the employer signals otherwise.
