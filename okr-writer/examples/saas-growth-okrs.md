# Worked Example: Company → Team OKR Cascade (B2B SaaS, Q3)

Context: A B2B SaaS company at $4.2M ARR wants to prove its self-serve motion can grow without heavy sales touch. Baselines were pulled from their analytics before drafting.

## Company Objective

**Objective: Prove self-serve can carry our growth.**  
*Type: Aspirational*

| # | Key Result | Baseline | Target |
|---|-----------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Grow self-serve ARR from $0.9M to $1.6M by Sept 30 | $0.9M | $1.6M |
| 2 | Increase trial→paid conversion from 4.0% to 7.0% | 4.0% | 7.0% |
| 3 | (Guardrail) Keep self-serve 90-day logo retention above 90% | 91% | ≥90% |

Initiatives (not KRs): launch in-product paywall, add usage-based pricing tier, build self-serve onboarding checklist.

## Product Team OKR (aligns to KR2 above)

**Objective: Make new users reach value before they lose interest.**  
*Type: Aspirational*

| # | Key Result | Baseline | Target |
|---|-----------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Raise day-1 activation (first successful project) from 38% to 60% | 38% | 60% |
| 2 | Cut median time-to-first-value from 3 days to under 4 hours | 3 days | <4h |
| 3 | (Guardrail) Keep onboarding-related support tickets per 100 signups below 8 | 11 | <8 |

Initiatives: rebuild onboarding wizard, add sample data templates, instrument the activation funnel.

## Growth/Marketing Team OKR (aligns to KR1 above)

**Objective: Turn our content into a predictable signup engine.**  
*Type: Committed*

| # | Key Result | Baseline | Target |
|---|-----------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Increase organic trial signups/week from 120 to 250 | 120 | 250 |
| 2 | Reduce blended CAC for self-serve from $180 to $120 | $180 | $120 |
| 3 | (Guardrail) Keep signup→activation above 38% (don't buy junk traffic) | 38% | ≥38% |

Initiatives: publish 2 SEO pillar pages/month, launch referral loop, A/B test signup page.

## Why this set works

- Each objective is qualitative and memorable; numbers live only in KRs.
- Every objective has a counter-balancing guardrail KR so teams can't game the headline.
- Team OKRs ladder to specific company KRs (alignment, not copy-paste).
- Baselines and deadlines are explicit; the set is gradable.
- Initiatives are listed separately and never confused with key results.
