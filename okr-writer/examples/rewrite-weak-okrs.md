# Before / After: Rewriting Weak OKRs

Each case shows the original, the diagnosis, and a stronger version.

## Case 1 — Task disguised as a KR

**Before**
- Objective: Improve onboarding
- KR1: Launch new onboarding flow
- KR2: Write help docs
- KR3: Add product tour

**Diagnosis:** All three KRs are outputs (inversion test fails — you could ship all three and activation could still drop). Objective is vague.

**After**
- Objective: Make new users succeed on their first day.
- KR1: Raise day-1 activation from 38% to 58%.
- KR2: Cut time-to-first-value from 3 days to under 4 hours.
- KR3 (guardrail): Keep onboarding support tickets per 100 signups below 8 (from 11).
- Initiatives: launch new flow, write docs, add product tour.

## Case 2 — Numbers in the objective, vanity metric

**Before**
- Objective: Get 1,000,000 page views
- KR1: Publish 40 blog posts
- KR2: Get 1M page views

**Diagnosis:** Objective is a metric (should be a KR). Page views are a vanity metric. Blog posts are an output.

**After**
- Objective: Make our content the top of the funnel for buyers.
- KR1: Increase content-sourced qualified leads from 60 to 200/month.
- KR2: Raise content→trial conversion from 1.2% to 3.0%.
- KR3 (guardrail): Keep lead-to-opportunity rate above 25%.
- Initiatives: publish 40 posts, refresh top 10 pages, add CTAs.

## Case 3 — Stitched objective, no baselines

**Before**
- Objective: Grow revenue, improve quality, and hire faster
- KR1: Increase revenue
- KR2: Fewer bugs
- KR3: Hire people

**Diagnosis:** Three goals stitched together; no baselines, no targets, no deadlines; KR3 is an activity.

**After (split into focused objectives)**

Objective A: Build a sales engine that scales beyond founders.
- KR: Grow new-business ARR from $1.1M to $1.8M by Q4.
- KR: Increase rep-sourced pipeline coverage from 2.1x to 3.0x.

Objective B: Earn customer trust through rock-solid quality.
- KR: Reduce P1 incidents from 6/quarter to ≤2.
- KR: Raise change-success rate from 88% to 97%.

(Hiring is an initiative supporting both, not an objective.)

## Case 4 — Sandbagged / business-as-usual

**Before**
- Objective: Keep the systems running
- KR1: Maintain 99.0% uptime (already at 99.4%)

**Diagnosis:** Target is below current performance — guaranteed 1.0, nothing learned.

**After**
- Objective: Make reliability a reason customers choose us.
- KR1: Improve uptime from 99.4% to 99.95%.
- KR2: Reduce MTTR from 45 min to under 10 min.
- KR3: Cut customer-reported incidents from 14 to ≤5/quarter.
