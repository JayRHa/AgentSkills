# Grocery List Organization

Consolidate identical ingredients across all meals, sum quantities, round up to purchasable package sizes, then sort into these sections **in this order** (matches a typical store flow, perimeter first):

1. Produce
2. Meat & Seafood
3. Dairy & Eggs
4. Deli & Prepared
5. Bakery & Bread
6. Frozen
7. Canned & Jarred
8. Dry Goods & Grains
9. Baking & Spices
10. Condiments, Oils & Sauces
11. Beverages
12. Snacks
13. Household & Misc

List **Pantry Staples (likely on hand)** as a separate trailing section so the user can tick off what they already own instead of re-buying.

## Ingredient -> section mapping (quick reference)

| Ingredient type | Section |
|---|---|
| Fresh fruit, vegetables, fresh herbs, garlic, onion | Produce |
| Chicken, beef, pork, fresh fish, shrimp | Meat & Seafood |
| Milk, cheese, yogurt, butter, eggs, cream | Dairy & Eggs |
| Tofu, hummus, rotisserie chicken, fresh pasta | Deli & Prepared |
| Bread, tortillas, pita, buns | Bakery & Bread |
| Frozen veg/fruit, frozen fish, ice | Frozen |
| Canned beans, tomatoes, tuna, coconut milk, broth | Canned & Jarred |
| Rice, oats, dried pasta, lentils, flour, quinoa | Dry Goods & Grains |
| Sugar, baking soda, salt, dried spices, yeast | Baking & Spices |
| Olive oil, soy sauce, vinegar, mustard, mayo, salsa | Condiments, Oils & Sauces |
| Coffee, tea, juice, sparkling water | Beverages |
| Nuts, crackers, chips, granola bars | Snacks |
| Foil, parchment, storage bags, dish soap | Household & Misc |

## Quantity rounding rules

- Convert fractional units to whole package buys: "1/2 onion x6 -> 3 onions".
- Use real package sizes: eggs by dozen/half-dozen; rice/oats by the bag; canned goods by the can.
- Combine duplicates: chicken in 3 meals -> sum grams, then convert to packs (e.g., "1.4 kg chicken thighs").
- Note when buying bulk is cheaper than the exact amount needed.

## Common pantry staples to call out separately

Salt, pepper, cooking oil, common dried spices, flour, sugar, soy sauce, vinegar, stock cubes, baking soda, common condiments. Assume the user may have these; list them under Pantry Staples so they confirm rather than auto-buy.
