# Competitive Data Sources & Ethics

Good analysis stands on legitimate, citable evidence. Collect openly, cite with dates, and never cross the lines below.

## Where to find legitimate intel

### Public, primary
- Competitor websites: product, pricing, docs, changelog, status page.
- Pricing and packaging pages (screenshot + date — they change often).
- Public roadmaps, release notes, and changelogs (momentum signal).
- Job postings — reveal tech stack, strategic bets, and team scaling.
- SEC/Companies-House filings, investor decks, earnings calls (for public firms).
- Press releases and the company blog.

### Third-party / aggregated
- Review platforms: G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, Gartner Peer Insights — read 1- and 2-star reviews for real weaknesses; note review count and recency.
- Analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester) where available.
- App store reviews and ratings (for mobile/desktop apps).
- Funding databases (Crunchbase, PitchBook) — funding = runway and intent.
- Web traffic / market-share estimators (Similarweb) — treat as directional `[est]`.
- Developer signals: GitHub stars/activity, package download counts, Stack Overflow presence.
- Social and community: subreddits, Discord/Slack communities, LinkedIn.

### Internal (often the best)
- Win/loss interviews and sales-call notes.
- Lost-deal CRM reasons.
- Customer interviews and support tickets mentioning competitors.
- Your own field sales team's anecdotes (verify before publishing).

## Citation discipline

- Record **source + URL + date accessed** for every non-obvious claim.
- Tag any inferred or modeled number as `[est]` with the basis ("[est] from Similarweb, May 2026").
- Prefer primary sources over secondary; prefer recent over old.
- Pricing, headcount, and roadmap claims expire fastest — date them precisely.

## Ethics & legal guardrails (do not cross)

- **Do not misrepresent yourself** to obtain confidential information (no fake prospect identities to extract internal pricing or roadmaps).
- **Do not access systems you're not entitled to** — no scraping behind authentication against ToS, no using leaked credentials, no circumventing paywalls.
- **Do not solicit a competitor's employees or customers to break NDAs.**
- **Do not pose as a customer** to abuse trials or support beyond normal evaluation.
- **Respect robots.txt and site Terms of Service** for any automated collection.
- **Honor confidentiality** of any NDA-covered material your company already holds; do not launder it into a public-facing deck.

Rule of thumb: if you'd be uncomfortable disclosing how you got the data, don't use it. Publicly available + honestly obtained + properly cited = safe.

## Freshness cadence

| Data type | Re-verify every |
|---|---|
| Pricing & packaging | Quarter |
| Feature set / roadmap | Quarter |
| Funding / headcount | 6 months |
| Positioning / messaging | 6 months |
| Win/loss themes | Ongoing |
