# Headlines & SEO

The headline does 80% of the work of getting the post read. Write 3-5 options, pick the most specific and benefit-driven one.

## Formulas that work for technical posts

| Formula | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| How-to | How to <achieve outcome> <constraint> | How to Cut Docker Image Size by 80% Without Multi-Stage Builds |
| Number/listicle | <N> <things> that <benefit> | 5 Postgres Indexes That Quietly Slow Down Writes |
| Lesson/story | What <event> Taught Me About <topic> | What a 14-Hour Outage Taught Me About Retries |
| Question | Why <surprising thing>? | Why Is Your Lambda Cold Start So Slow? |
| Versus | <A> vs <B>: <decision angle> | gRPC vs REST: When the Hype Is Actually Right |
| Contrarian | Stop <common practice> | Stop Mocking the Database in Your Tests |
| Outcome | <Outcome> in <timeframe/effort> | Zero-Downtime Migrations in 200 Lines |

## Rules
- **Be specific.** "Faster builds" → "4-minute builds." Specifics signal real content.
- **Lead with the benefit or the surprise**, not the tooling, unless the tool is the draw.
- **Numbers help** — odd numbers and percentages perform well and feel concrete.
- **Length:** aim 50-60 characters so it isn't truncated in search results. Hard cap ~70.
- **One promise.** Don't cram two ideas into the title.
- **Avoid hype words** with no backing: "ultimate," "revolutionary," "blazing" — unless you prove them.
- **Match search intent** for SEO posts: include the term people would actually type.

## Meta description
- 150-160 characters.
- Restate the benefit and include the primary keyword once.
- Treat it as a second headline — it's the search-result subtitle.
- Example: "Three concrete changes that took our CI pipeline from 22 to 4 minutes, including the exact GitHub Actions config and what to measure first."

## URL slug
- Short, lowercase, hyphenated, keyword-forward: `cut-ci-pipeline-time`.
- Drop stop words (the, a, to) where it still reads.

## Headline self-check
1. Could a competitor's post have the exact same title? Make it more specific.
2. Does it promise something the post delivers? No bait.
3. Read it aloud — does it sound like a human said it?
