# Pre-Send Checklist

Run through this before sending. The starred items are non-negotiable.

## Purpose & Clarity
- [ ] *The single primary ask is identifiable in the first 2 sentences (BLUF).
- [ ] One email = one primary ask. Secondary items are clearly secondary.
- [ ] A first-time reader understands what this is about in under 10 seconds.

## Call to Action
- [ ] *There is an explicit CTA — a specific action, not "let me know".
- [ ] *The CTA names an owner (who does it).
- [ ] *The CTA has a deadline or timeframe when one is relevant.
- [ ] Replying is easy: concrete options offered where possible (e.g., two time slots).

## Subject Line
- [ ] Specific and scannable; keyword front-loaded.
- [ ] Under ~60 characters; includes deadline if one exists.
- [ ] Not a banned filler ("Hi", "Quick question", "Touching base").

## Tone
- [ ] Formality matches the relationship and seniority.
- [ ] Warmth is appropriate to the situation (favor/bad news -> warmer).
- [ ] No passive-aggression ("per my last email"), no over-apologizing.
- [ ] Hedging trimmed: not more than one softener per ask.

## Structure & Length
- [ ] Greeting and sign-off present and matched in formality.
- [ ] Paragraphs are short; white space and bullets used for scannability.
- [ ] The one key line is emphasized (bold) when helpful.
- [ ] Under ~150 words unless the content genuinely needs more; if >200 words or >3 back-and-forths, consider a call/doc instead.

## Accuracy & Hygiene
- [ ] Recipient name(s) and any honorifics spelled correctly.
- [ ] Dates are unambiguous and include a time zone for deadlines.
- [ ] All promised attachments/links are actually included.
- [ ] Names of people/companies/products are correct.
- [ ] No confidential info to the wrong audience; check the To/Cc/Bcc lines.
- [ ] Reply-all is intentional, not accidental.

## Final
- [ ] Read aloud once. If a line sounds wrong said out loud, fix it.
