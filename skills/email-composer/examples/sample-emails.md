# Sample Emails — Before / After

Nine worked examples. Each shows a weak draft and a rewritten version applying the skill.

---

## 1. Request
**Before**
> Subject: Question
>
> Hey, hope you're well! I was wondering if you might possibly have some time at some point to maybe take a look at the budget when you get a chance? Thanks so much!!

**After**
> Subject: Approval needed: Q3 budget by Thu Jun 12
>
> Hi Dana,
>
> The Q3 budget draft is ready and matches what we agreed in last week's planning call.
>
> Could you review and approve it by **Thursday, Jun 12 (5pm CET)** so finance can lock it for the quarter? It's a two-page summary — link below.
>
> Happy to walk you through it on a quick call if easier.
>
> Thanks,
> Jannik

Why: specific subject + deadline, BLUF, one clear ask with owner and date, hedging removed.

## 2. Follow-up (gentle)
**Before**
> Subject: Re: Re: Re: contract
>
> Per my last email, I still need this.

**After**
> Subject: Reminder: MSA signature by Fri Jun 13
>
> Hi Priya,
>
> Floating this back up — were you able to e-sign the MSA from my note on Monday?
>
> A one-line reply on timing would help me plan onboarding. No rush if you're swamped.
>
> Thanks,
> Jannik

Why: drops passive-aggression, restates the single ask, easy CTA, graceful out.

## 3. Follow-up (firmer)
**After**

>
> Hi Priya,
>
> Following up again on the MSA signature. We need it by **Friday, Jun 13** to start onboarding on the 16th as planned.
>
> Can you confirm that timeline works? If something's holding it up on your side, tell me and I'll help clear it.

## 4. Decline

>
> Ahh I'd love to but I'm soooo busy, sorry, maybe another time?? :(

**After**
> Subject: Re: speaking at DevConf — not this round
>
> Hi Marco,
>
> Thank you for the invite to speak at DevConf — I'm flattered you thought of me.
>
> I can't take it on this quarter; my schedule is fully committed and I'd rather decline than under-prepare. Could my colleague Lena, who leads our platform work, be a fit? Happy to introduce you.

## 5. Apology
**Before**
> Subject: sorry
>
> So so sorry about the report being late, I feel terrible, sorry again, it won't happen again I promise sorry.

**After**
> Subject: Apology + fix: late analytics report
>
> Hi Tom,
>
> I'm sorry the analytics report missed Monday's deadline — that delayed your board prep, and that's on me.
>
> The corrected report is attached now. To prevent a repeat, I've added a Friday checkpoint to the workflow so it's ready a day early going forward.
>
> Thanks for your patience.
> Jannik

Why: owns it once, concrete fix + safeguard, no groveling.

## 8. Cold outreach
**Before**
> Subject: Partnership Opportunity!!!
>
> Dear Sir/Madam, I am reaching out to explore synergies between our organizations...

**After**
> Subject: Your post on cutting CI costs 40%
>
> Hi Renee,
>
> Your write-up on cutting CI costs 40% with build caching hit home — we're wrestling with the same bill at Epic Fusion.
>
> I lead platform engineering here; we've built tooling that trims test-suite time on top of caching.
>
> Would a 15-minute call next week be worth it to compare notes? If you're not the right person, could you point me to who is?
>
> Thanks,
> Jannik

Why: researched hook, one line of credibility, low-friction CTA with a fallback.
