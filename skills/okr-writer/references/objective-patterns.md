# Objective Patterns

An **Objective** is qualitative, memorable, inspirational, and time-bound to the period. It contains **no metrics** — numbers live in the Key Results.

## Properties of a Strong Objective

- Short enough to recite from memory (aim for under 12 words).
- Describes a desirable end state, not an activity.
- Inspiring or meaningful to the people doing the work.
- Single-focus — not two goals stitched with "and".
- Owned by a clear team or person.

## Sentence Patterns

Use these as scaffolds, then make them specific and human.

1. **Become / Be the X** — "Become the fastest checkout in e-commerce."
2. **Make X love/trust Y** — "Make first-time buyers trust us enough to return."
3. **Win / Own [market or moment]** — "Own the SMB onboarding experience."
4. **Transform X into Y** — "Transform support from a cost center into a growth driver."
5. **Delight by doing X** — "Delight users with an app that never makes them wait."
6. **Reach a milestone state** — "Reach product-market fit in the German market."

## Objective Bank: Strong vs Weak

| Weak (why) | Strong rewrite |
|------------|----------------|
| "Increase revenue by 20%" (that's a KR) | "Build a sales engine that scales beyond founders" |
| "Improve the product" (vague) | "Make our app the one customers recommend to friends" |
| "Ship the redesign and improve performance and hire" (stitched) | Split into three single-focus objectives |
| "Do marketing" (an activity) | "Become a brand SMBs discover before they search" |
| "Q3 goals" (not a goal) | "Turn trial users into paying advocates" |
| "Be better than competitors" (unmotivating, vague) | "Be the obvious choice for first-time data teams" |

## Anti-Patterns

- **Numbers in the objective** — move them to the KRs.
- **Buzzword soup** — "Leverage synergies to optimize stakeholder value." Say it plainly.
- **Business-as-usual** — "Keep the lights on." Maintenance is not an objective; if reliability matters, frame it aspirationally ("Earn customer trust through rock-solid reliability").
- **Too many** — more than 3-5 objectives per team means no priorities.
