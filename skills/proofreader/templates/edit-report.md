## Summary

- **Variant assumed:** US / UK / other (state it)

## Applied Fixes (objective)

Group by category. Show original → revised for non-trivial changes.

### Grammar & Mechanics
- "<original>" → "<revised>" — reason (e.g., subject–verb agreement)

### Concision
- "<wordy original>" → "<tight revised>" — removed redundancy/filler

### Consistency
- Standardized <X> to <Y> throughout (e.g., "colour" → "color")

## Flags (need author input)

- **Ambiguity:** "<sentence>" could mean (a) … or (b) … — which did you intend?
