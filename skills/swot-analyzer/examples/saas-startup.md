# Worked Example: SWOT for a B2B SaaS Startup

**Subject:** Northbeam, a 25-person B2B analytics SaaS.
**Decision this serves:** Decide the FY26 strategy — push upmarket to enterprise, or double down on SMB.
**Time frame:** Next 12 months.

## 1. SWOT Grid (top items, scored Impact x Conf)

### Strengths
| # | Factor | Imp | Conf | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Fastest time-to-value in category: median 2 days to first dashboard vs. 3-4 weeks for incumbents | 5 | 5 | 25 |
| S2 | Net revenue retention 118% (strong expansion within accounts) | 5 | 4 | 20 |
| S3 | Proprietary data-normalization engine, 2 patents pending | 4 | 4 | 16 |

### Weaknesses
| # | Factor | Imp | Conf | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | No SOC 2 Type II / SSO — blocks ~70% of enterprise deals | 5 | 5 | 25 |
| W2 | Revenue concentration: top customer = 22% of ARR | 4 | 5 | 20 |
| W3 | Thin sales team (2 AEs); founder-led sales doesn't scale | 4 | 4 | 16 |

### Opportunities
| # | Factor (external + horizon) | Imp | Lik | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | Incumbent "DataCorp" sunsetting its low-tier plan (now-6mo) — strands thousands of SMBs | 5 | 4 | 20 |
| O2 | Surge in demand for AI-assisted analytics; buyers actively budgeting (now-12mo) | 4 | 4 | 16 |
| O3 | New EU reporting regulation creates compliance-driven analytics demand (6-18mo) | 3 | 3 | 9 |

### Threats
| # | Factor (external + horizon) | Imp | Lik | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Well-funded entrant "Pulse" raised $40M, copying our onboarding UX (now-12mo) | 5 | 4 | 20 |
| T2 | Enterprise buyers consolidating vendors; prefer suites over point tools (12-24mo) | 4 | 3 | 12 |
| T3 | Macro budget tightening lengthening sales cycles ~30% (now-12mo) | 3 | 4 | 12 |

## 2. TOWS Strategy Matrix

### S-O — Attack
- **[S1 + O1]** Run a "DataCorp refugee" migration campaign: one-click importer + 2-day onboarding promise to capture stranded SMBs before competitors react.
- **[S3 + O2]** Ship an AI-insights layer on the normalization engine to ride the AI-analytics budget wave with a defensible data advantage.

### S-T — Defend
- **[S2 + T1]** Lean on 118% NRR: deepen product expansion features so Pulse's UX clone can't peel accounts — switching cost beats look-alike onboarding.
- **[S3 + T2]** Position the patented engine as an embeddable layer; partner with suites rather than be displaced by them.

### W-O — Improve
- **[W1 + O1/O3]** Fast-track SOC 2 Type II + SSO to unlock enterprise and EU-compliance demand currently blocked.

### W-T — Survive
- **[W2 + T3]** Diversify off the 22%-concentration customer before macro pressure threatens that renewal: accelerate SMB pipeline (which O1 conveniently fuels).
- **[W3 + T1]** Hire 2 AEs + a sales lead now; founder-led sales can't outrun a $40M-funded competitor.

## 3. Action Plan

| # | Action | Why (TOWS) | Owner | Horizon | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Launch DataCorp migration campaign + importer | S1+O1 | Head of Growth | Now | 150 migrated SMB accounts in 90d |
| A2 | Reduce top-customer ARR share below 12% | W2+T3 | CEO/Sales | 90 days | Concentration <12%; renewal signed |
| A3 | Hire 2 AEs + sales lead | W3+T1 | CEO | 90 days | Pipeline coverage 3x; founder off deals |
| A4 | Achieve SOC 2 Type II + ship SSO | W1+O1 | CTO | 12 months | Audit passed; 5 enterprise logos |
| A5 | Ship AI-insights beta | S3+O2 | Head of Product | 12 months | 30% of accounts using; +5pts NRR |

_Sequencing: A2 (survival) and A1/A3 (capture the time-boxed O1 window) first; A4/A5 build the next wave._

## 4. Strategic Posture & Recommendation

**Posture:** Aggressive in SMB, build toward enterprise. Weight sits in S+O (strong onboarding/NRR meeting a time-boxed competitor exit), so offense is warranted — but a real W-T survival risk (customer concentration + macro) must be capped first.

**Recommendation:** Do NOT chase enterprise yet — W1 (no SOC 2) blocks it and the SMB opportunity (O1) is time-boxed and plays directly to S1. Win the DataCorp land grab now, de-risk concentration, and run the enterprise enablement (A4) in parallel for FY27.

**Single most important next move:** Ship the DataCorp migration importer and launch the campaign within 30 days — the window closes when DataCorp completes its sunset.
